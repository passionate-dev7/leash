Press run. A fresh agent key is authorized on Tempo's Moderato testnet with a budget priced from live MPP 402 challenges, it pays real MPP payout addresses until the budget is gone, and then the chain refuses it four different ways. Nothing below is simulated: every refusal is a response from a node, every balance is read back from the AccountKeychain precompile, and the kill switch is shown with the ugly timing it actually has.

  • ~60 seconds. Roughly 10 transactions, most of them under 2 s.
  • Budgets come from getRemainingLimitWithPeriod. Never from the event stream, which is stale by the gas refund.
  • MPP settles on mainnet 4217. Payout addresses are resolved live there and enforced here on 42431, in a currency those same challenges quote.
